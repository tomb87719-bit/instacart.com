Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Order & Delivery Help information website (“this website”) handles information when you browse its pages.

1. Information you provide

This website provides general informational guides. It does not ask visitors to create an account or submit Home Depot order details, payment card details, passwords, or other retailer account credentials through the guide pages. Please do not enter sensitive personal or payment information into this website.

2. Automatically collected technical information

When you visit a website, the website host or server may process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and request timestamps for security, delivery, and troubleshooting. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and its settings. This website does not itself claim to collect analytics or advertising profile data.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The guide pages are designed to work as informational web pages. If the hosting provider or any third-party service used by the site sets cookies or similar technologies, their use is governed by that provider’s settings and policies. No account sign-in is provided on this website.

4. Links to other websites

This website may link to external websites, including The Home Depot’s website. External websites operate independently and have their own privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of an external website before providing information there.

5. Phone assistance

The phone number displayed on this website is for assistance with this informational website only. If you choose to call, the phone service provider may process call-related information under its own privacy practices. This website cannot access retailer customer accounts, order records, or payment information.

6. Children’s privacy

This website is general informational content and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when this page was most recently revised.

8. Contact

For questions about this information website or this policy, use the website assistance contact details displayed on the home page.
