Step-by-Step Help Guides

Open a topic to see general information and suggested next steps.

How to check an order status
  1. Go to the retailer's official website.
  2. Sign in using the account used to place the order.
  3. Open your order history and select the relevant order.
  4. Review the latest status and estimated dates displayed there.
How to review delivery tracking
  1. Find the shipping confirmation from the retailer.
  2. Open the tracking link provided in that message.
  3. Review the latest carrier scan and estimated delivery window.
  4. If tracking appears delayed, contact the retailer or carrier through its official support channel.
What to prepare before a return
  1. Review the retailer's current return policy and item eligibility.
  2. Locate your receipt or order confirmation.
  3. Check the packaging and condition requirements.
  4. Follow the official return instructions supplied by the retailer.
General refund information
  1. Check the order page for the latest return or refund status.
  2. Review the retailer's published refund policy.
  3. Keep any return confirmation or tracking reference.
  4. For the status of a specific refund, contact the retailer directly.
Payment and billing basics
  1. Review the order confirmation for the payment method used.
  2. Compare the charge with the order total and any separate shipment charges.
  3. Never share full card numbers, passwords, or one-time security codes with this website.
  4. Contact the retailer or your payment provider about a specific charge.
Account and sign-in basics
  1. Make sure you are visiting the retailer's official website.
  2. Check that you are using the correct email address or phone number.
  3. Use the official password-reset process if you cannot sign in.
  4. Do not share your password or verification codes.
Product support and warranty information

Check the product documentation and the manufacturer's published warranty terms. For a specific warranty claim, contact the retailer or manufacturer through its official channels.

Store pickup information

Review the pickup instructions in the order confirmation. Check the selected store, pickup status, and any identification requirements listed by the retailer.

Delivery options and scheduling

Available delivery options and scheduling depend on the item, address, and retailer terms. Review the options shown during checkout or in your order details.

Changing or cancelling an order

Order changes and cancellations may only be available for a limited time. Check the order details on the retailer's official website and contact its support team promptly.

Installation and assembly guidance

Review the product instructions and safety information before beginning a project. For installation bookings or service changes, use the retailer's official service channels.

Service and rental information

Service availability, rental terms, deposits, and scheduling vary by location. Check the retailer's official service pages for current details.

Please note: These are general informational guides. This independent website cannot access customer accounts, look up orders, change or cancel purchases, issue refunds, or arrange retailer services. For account-specific assistance, use The Home Depot's official website.
