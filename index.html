Frequently asked questions

Are you the official Starlink support team?

No. This is an independent third-party assistance service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Starlink or SpaceX. For account, billing, warranty or service-plan matters, use Starlink's official website and support channels.

What kind of help can I request?

We provide general guidance on setup, router and Wi-Fi configuration, and common connectivity troubleshooting.

Can you access or change my Starlink account?

No. We do not claim official account access. Do not share your account password, payment details, or one-time verification codes with anyone.

Can you guarantee a particular internet speed?

No. Internet performance depends on factors such as service availability, equipment, environment and network conditions. We do not guarantee speeds, coverage or uptime.
