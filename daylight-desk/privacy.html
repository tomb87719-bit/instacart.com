Your privacy
matters here.

This page explains what happens to information when you use Daylight Desk.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 14, 2026

Information you enter

Tasks and habit check-ins you create are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. This static website does not transmit those entries to a Daylight Desk server. The focus timer runs in your browser and does not save your session.

Accounts and payments

This version of Daylight Desk does not offer account registration, sign-in, or payment processing. It does not ask you to provide a name, password, or payment details.

Third-party services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. You can block external font requests using your browser settings; the site will fall back to system fonts.

Hosting and server logs

When you host this website, your hosting provider may process standard technical request data (such as IP address, requested page, and browser information) in server logs for security and operation. Check your chosen hosting provider's privacy terms for its practices.

Your choices

You can remove tasks and habits within the tools, or clear this website's local storage/site data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data may permanently erase your saved entries.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's features or hosting arrangements change. Please review it occasionally.

Contact

For privacy questions, add your preferred contact details here before publishing the website. Do not publish placeholder contact information.
