A NOTE ABOUT US

Less noise.
More of what matters.

Daylight Desk is a small collection of practical tools for ordinary days.

Why Daylight Desk?

Some days feel full before they even begin. Daylight Desk was made to offer a little breathing room: a simple place to plan, focus, and keep track of the small routines that help a day feel good.

Three tools, one gentle idea

The task planner helps you get the next steps out of your head. The focus timer gives one task a little protected time. The habit tracker helps you notice the small things you repeat. Use one, use all three, or come back whenever you need a fresh start.

Private by design

Your tasks and habit check-ins are stored locally in your browser. This static website does not require an account or send your entries to a server. Read the Privacy Policy for more details.

Make it your own

There is no perfect way to use these tools. Start with one small thing, and let the rest follow.
