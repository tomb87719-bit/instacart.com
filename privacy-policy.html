Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. About this website

This Privacy Policy explains how Starlink Setup Help handles information when you visit this website. The website provides general information about independent setup guidance and connectivity troubleshooting assistance.

2. Information you provide

This website currently does not contain an account-registration form, contact form, payment form, or newsletter sign-up. If you choose to call the customer assistance number displayed on the website, your call and any information you choose to share will be handled through your telephone and network providers and by the person or service receiving the call.

Please do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, payment card details, or other sensitive account credentials over an ordinary phone call.

3. Technical information

When you visit a website, the hosting provider and related infrastructure may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and access times for security, reliability, and troubleshooting. The specific logs retained depend on the hosting provider and its settings.

4. Cookies and analytics

The website code provided for this site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. External services used to load fonts or images may receive technical request information when your browser loads those resources. Your browser settings can help you manage cookies and other site data.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by the website hosting provider and infrastructure providers to deliver and secure the website. Retention periods depend on the applicable provider settings and operational requirements.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to call the assistance number and can use browser settings to limit cookies or block external resources. If you have a privacy question about information you shared during a call, use the contact number on the home page and identify the information you are asking about. Do not include passwords or verification codes.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Customer Assistance at +1 (920) 345-7857.
