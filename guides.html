⌂Starlink Setup & InstallationPrepare your equipment and understand the setup sequence›

Before you begin

  • Check that the kit includes the items listed for your model.
  • Choose a stable location with a wide, unobstructed view of the sky.
  • Keep cables protected from sharp edges, water and trip hazards.

General setup sequence

  1. Place the dish on a stable surface or install the approved mount according to the product instructions.
  2. Connect the supplied cable and power equipment as described in the model's official setup guide.
  3. Power on the system and allow it time to start.
  4. Use the official app's prompts to check placement and complete the setup steps.

Exact steps vary by hardware generation. Follow the instructions supplied with your equipment.

◉Wi-Fi & Router SettingsConnect devices and understand wireless settings›

Connecting a device

  1. Open Wi-Fi settings on your phone, computer or smart TV.
  2. Select your network name (SSID).
  3. Enter the Wi-Fi password you created during setup.
  4. If the device does not connect, check the password and restart its Wi-Fi connection.

If Wi-Fi is not working

  • Check that the router has power and its cables are firmly connected.
  • Move closer to the router to test signal strength.
  • Restart the device that cannot connect.
  • Check the router manufacturer's guidance before changing advanced settings.
▣Starlink App GuideUnderstand app setup, prompts and status information›

Getting started

  1. Install the official Starlink app from your device's app store.
  2. Open it and follow the sign-in or setup instructions shown by the app.
  3. Review any placement, obstruction or connection prompts.
  4. Keep the app updated to access current features.

App screens and available options can change with software updates and equipment model.

⚒No Internet / TroubleshootingWork through common connection problems›

Basic checks

  1. Check whether the service has a reported outage using the official app or service-status information.
  2. Inspect power and data cables for loose connections or visible damage.
  3. Check the app for alerts or obstruction messages.
  4. Restart equipment only by following the instructions for your model.
  5. If the issue continues, note any error message and contact official support for account- or service-specific help.
▥Speed, Performance & LagUnderstand slow speeds, buffering and latency›

Things to check

  • Compare performance near the router and farther away to identify a Wi-Fi coverage issue.
  • Pause large downloads on other devices while testing.
  • Use a current device and browser for a consistent test.
  • Check for obstructions or service alerts in the official app.
  • Repeat a speed test at different times and record the results.

Actual speeds and latency vary with network conditions, location, plan, equipment and other factors. No specific performance level is guaranteed.

⌖Dish Placement & ObstructionsChoose a suitable location and understand obstructions›

Placement tips

  • Choose a location with a broad, clear view of the sky.
  • Nearby trees, buildings, poles and roof features may interrupt the signal.
  • Use the official app's obstruction tool, where available, to assess the proposed location.
  • Make sure the mount is secure and installation follows local safety requirements.

Do not climb onto a roof or work near power lines unless you are properly qualified and equipped.

▣Equipment, Cables & HardwareIdentify kit components and basic checks›

Basic equipment checks

  • Identify your dish, router, power supply and cables using the product documentation.
  • Check cables and connectors for damage, moisture or debris.
  • Use only compatible power supplies and accessories recommended for your equipment.
  • Do not open, modify or attempt to repair powered hardware yourself.
▤Billing, Plans & Account QuestionsKnow where to find official account information›

Where to check

  • Use the official Starlink website or app to review your plan, invoices and payment methods.
  • Check the account email for billing notifications.
  • For payment failures, refunds, plan changes or account access, contact official Starlink support directly.

Never share your password, payment card details or one-time verification codes with anyone offering general setup assistance.
